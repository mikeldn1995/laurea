I combine developing coding skills with AI tools to build these projects. Please consider this work as evidence of prior learning.

Together, these projects connect a digital credit shop, audience-focused content, technical SEO, business-specific conversion analysis and supported customer journeys to the five AHOT learning outcomes.

1.

Building an online shop and integrating it with a website

Brandit's public website, https://www.branditlondon.com/, presents branding, visibility and operations services and links to the client workspace and a free 30-minute call form. Token bundles are offered inside the practitioner workspace, through Stripe Checkout. Purchase records, payment verification and webhook fulfilment connect payment to a usable invoicing balance. The homepage does not advertise token bundles.

2.

Driving traffic to the online shop

Service, audience and location pages and published articles support organic discovery and routes to an enquiry. Brandit's public offer links visitors to a call request and client workspace. Alexander's traffic is analysed below. Alicia's recent launch means SEO growth cannot yet be assessed.

3.

Optimizing SEO for the online shop

Alicia's site has page-specific titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots rules and practice, practitioner and location structured data. Brandit's public site also uses titles, descriptions, canonical and sharing metadata. These are implemented foundations for search discovery.

4.

Analyzing relevant conversions for various online businesses

Alexander's Cloudflare Overview reported about 1.67k unique visitors and 21.6k requests for 19 September-19 October 2026. Its IP-based visitor count can include bots and is not verified human sessions. Resend showed four delivered emails for 5-19 October: two introductory-call request notifications and two visitor acknowledgements. That supports two submitted request notifications, not four leads or confirmed bookings. I cannot calculate a conversion rate from these differing periods and denominators. For Brandit, a suitable measure is distinct verified paid sessions divided by distinct created checkout sessions in a dated cohort.

5.

Supporting the customer's buying process

Brandit recommends sufficient tokens, shows prices and balances, and preserves prepared invoices during checkout. Verified payment triggers fulfilment. The service websites help visitors decide to enquire; Alexander's acknowledgement confirms receipt, while the therapist notification supports follow-up.
